Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Raz Burg AudioCrafts ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit audiocrafts.razburg.com (the "Site"), create an account, or download our software. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Who we are

The Site and the software are provided by Raz Burg, a sole proprietor (licensed dealer, osek murshe) registered in Israel, trading as Raz Burg AudioCrafts. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can contact us through the contact form at razburg.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash by our authentication provider; we cannot see them.
  • Library and licence data: which products you added to My plugins, when you added them, and the iLok activation codes assigned to you.
  • Download and usage data: which installers you download and when, and authentication events such as sign-ins, password resets and email confirmations.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and similar data recorded automatically in the server logs of our hosting and authentication providers, used for security and to keep the Site working.
  • Communications: anything you send us when you contact us.

We do not collect payment details, and we do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To provide your account and the software you request: sign-in, your library, activation codes and downloads. Basis: performance of our agreement with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To send service emails such as email confirmation and password reset. Basis: performance of our agreement.
  • To protect the Site and our software: preventing abuse, fraud, multiple accounts used to obtain extra licences, and unauthorised distribution. Basis: our legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with the law and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests.
  • To send news about our products, only if you separately agree. You can withdraw that consent at any time. Basis: consent (art. 6(1)(a)).

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

4. Cookies and local storage

The Site uses only strictly necessary storage: your browser's local storage keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie (rba_at, valid for up to 10 minutes) authorises installer downloads. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Because this storage is essential to the service you request, it does not require consent.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf under data processing terms, and only as needed:

  • Supabase, Inc.: authentication, database and file storage (servers in the European Union).
  • Netlify, Inc.: website hosting and content delivery.
  • Our email delivery provider: sending service emails.
  • PACE Anti-Piracy, Inc. (iLok): when you redeem an activation code in iLok License Manager, PACE processes your iLok account data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose data if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers are based in the United States or other countries outside Israel and the European Economic Area. Where data is transferred, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, and on transfers permitted under the Israeli Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and library data: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted from our active systems straight away and from provider backups within their normal backup cycle.
  • Records of which activation code was issued: kept after account deletion, without your email address, so that the same code is never issued twice.
  • Server and security logs: kept by our providers for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.
  • Data we need for a legal claim or obligation: for as long as that requires.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, row-level access controls so that each user can only read their own data, and short-lived download links. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the competent authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data. You can delete your account yourself at any time from My plugins;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent;
  • data portability;
  • complain to a supervisory authority. In Israel this is the Privacy Protection Authority; in the EU, the data protection authority where you live.

To exercise these rights, contact us through razburg.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will reply within the time required by law (normally within 30 days).

10. Children

The Site is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old, or the age of digital consent in your country if higher, to create an account. If we learn that we hold data of a child below that age, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, if the changes are significant, notify account holders by email or on the Site.
