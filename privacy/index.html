Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our account provider; we never see them.
  • Your plugin library: which plugins you added and the iLok activation codes assigned to you.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the service secure.

Why we use it

  • To let you sign in, keep your library, give you your activation codes and installers.
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.

We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores accounts, plugin libraries and installers.
  • Netlify hosts this website.
  • iLok (PACE Anti-Piracy) handles plugin activation when you redeem a code in iLok License Manager, under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can delete your account at any time from My plugins. This removes your email and library from our systems. Plugins you already activated stay in your iLok account.
  • For any other privacy request, contact us through razburg.com.
