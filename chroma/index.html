Features

Everything a compressor needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Classic controls up front, with colour and sidechain tools one click away.

Character knob

Six voices with their own detector, knee, release and saturation, switchable while you play.

Saturation in voice

Each character saturates its own way, from silky tube to gritty transistor, on its own knob that works at any Parallel setting.

Soft Clip and Parallel

Catch peaks at the output and blend the dry signal back in for New York style punch.

Key sidechain

Use your host's sidechain input, or Freq Key from 50 Hz to 16 kHz, with Listen.

Zero latency

Safe on tracking, on the mix bus and anywhere in between.

Built for sessions

29 presets, 100-step undo and redo, reset and a built-in user guide.

Characters

Six voices on one knob.

Each character changes the detector, the knee, the release behaviour and the colour of its saturation. Turn it while the track plays.

Vintage

Optical-tube leveling with a smooth, program-dependent release.

Retro

Fast transistor punch, bright and forward.

Chubby

Variable-tube thickness, gentle at first and firmer the harder you push.

Indie

Raw and gritty. Past 10:1 it over-compresses for pumping effects.

Modern

Clean and precise, for transparent control.

Bus

Mix-bus glue with a dual release.
