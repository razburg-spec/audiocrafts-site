About

Raz Burg

Twenty-five years of listening, built into tools.

Sound designer, producer, recording and mix engineer. The man behind AudioCrafts, and the ears every one of its tools has to get past.

The long apprenticeship

For more than twenty-five years, Raz Burg has lived on the working side of the glass.

As a sound designer, a producer and a recording and mix engineer, he has carried hundreds of projects from the first take to the final print. Songs and soundscapes, voices and textures, records that needed to breathe and records that needed to hit. Each one asked a slightly different question, and each one left something behind: a habit of the ear, a reflex of the hand, a firm opinion about how a sound should move.

A memory of machines

Along the way he came to know the instruments of the craft not as names on a front panel but as personalities. The tape that rounds a transient before you notice it happened. The light cell that lets go a little more slowly every time it works. The console whose bell tightens as you lean on it. The transformer that adds weight only where weight belongs. The early digital box whose grain turned, over the years, into a colour of its own.

Every era of audio left a fingerprint, and so did every way of working: how engineers in one decade pushed a chain, how another decade learned to hold back. He learned to hear those fingerprints the way a winemaker tastes a valley.

Every era of audio left a fingerprint. These tools are what happens when you stop choosing between them.

The itch

Somewhere in that long conversation with equipment, a question started to burn. What if the best of those characters did not have to live apart? What if the patience of one decade’s leveler could meet the bite of another’s, inside a single tool, voiced by someone who had spent a lifetime learning what each of them is good for?

That desire, to turn what the studio had taught his ears into instruments that other people could play, is where AudioCrafts began.

Sophistication behind the curtain

The idea is simple to say and hard to do. Up front, every AudioCrafts tool is direct and intuitive: a handful of controls, a clear display, nothing standing between the engineer and the decision. Behind the curtain run sound engines of real depth, built on original blends of character drawn from different machines and different eras. Never a replica of a single unit. Always a new recipe, tuned by ear until it earns its place.

The result is colour and personality that feel familiar in the best possible way, and belong to nothing else.

Made at the console, for the console

None of this is a laboratory exercise. Raz uses these tools in his own mixing and sound design, and today they also sit in the sessions of engineers and producers around the world. Each one stays on the bench until it sounds right on a real mix. Only then does it leave the studio.

A personal note

“I made these tools with a lot of love, and I invite you to work with them the same way. Put them on your tracks, push them, break the rules with them, and make them yours. And please tell me what you hear. Every comment, every review and every idea is welcome. It is how the next version gets better.”

Raz BurgFounder, Raz Burg AudioCrafts
