Lumen

Every era of space, in one plugin.

A creative space processor that blends lush reverbs, warm analog-style delays and a rising shimmer reverb into one instrument. It opens fully dry, so nothing changes until you switch an engine on.

Features

Three engines that play together.

Reverb, delay and shimmer each have their own section, with a shared view that shows exactly what the tail is doing.

Thirty reverb flavours

Hall, Plate, Room, Chamber, Bloom and Cloud, each in five eras from the 60s to now.

Size without pitch bends

Change the room size freely. It never bends the pitch: the old tail simply rings out.

Coloration

One knob from warm Dark to bright Shine harmonics, neutral in the middle.

Analog-style delay

Tempo-synced echoes with analog, tape and lo-fi character, Freeze and wide stereo modes.

Shimmer reverb

Pitch shifting inside its own lush reverb loop, so the tail climbs octave by octave into a rising, choir-like wash. Two intervals, Smooth or Reverse voice.

Built for sessions

47 presets in 9 categories, 100-step undo and redo, one-click reset and a built-in user guide.

Reverb modes × eras

Pick a room. Then pick a decade.

Every mode works in every era, so a Plate can sound like a dark 70s plate or a clean modern one. Eras are level-matched.

60s

Tape-fed chambers with soft, rounded tails.

70s

Dark, dense plates with a warm top end.

80s

12-bit halls with grainy, glossy tails.

90s

Lush digital spaces with wide, smooth modulation.

Now

Clean, precise modern space with full bandwidth.

Six modes

Hall, Plate, Room, Chamber, Bloom and Cloud, in any era.
