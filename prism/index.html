Six EQs. The same controls.

Draw your curve once, then switch modes to hear it in a different voice: broad and warm, silky, thick, punchy, clean or polished for the master. The controls never change, so nothing gets confusing. It opens flat and leaves your audio untouched until you move something.

Features

See what you hear. Hear it six ways.

A classic six-band layout with a display that shows exactly what the EQ is doing to your track.

Six modes

Each mode reshapes the bells, the shelves and the filters the way its family of classic EQs does, and has its own analog stage.

Live spectrum

The source before the EQ in gray, the result after it in color, with the EQ curve drawn on top.

Drag to shape

Drag a point for frequency and gain, scroll for Q, double-click to switch a band on or off.

Cut or shelf

The low and high bands work as a cut from 12 to 48 dB per octave or as a shelf.

Drive

Push the analog stage of the selected mode for harmonics, weight and softness. At zero the path stays completely clean.

Built for sessions

Zero latency, curves that keep their shape right up to 20 kHz, presets, 100-step undo and redo and a built-in user guide.

Modes

One curve. Six voices.

The same names as Chroma’s characters, so the family sounds and feels the same. Switch while the track plays.

Vintage

Passive tube warmth. Broad, gentle curves; low boosts scoop the mud just above them.

Retro

70s console silk. Bells start wide and tighten as you push, with sweet, forward mids.

Chubby

Class-A iron. Fat lows with a resonant shelf, broad musical bells and thick harmonics.

Indie

Large-format console bite. Tight bells, punchy shelves and a gritty edge.

Modern

Clean and exact. Transparent, with an open top end and no added color.

Bus

Mastering polish. Smooth, wide curves, gentle air and subtle harmonic glue.
